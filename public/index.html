How it works
After uploading, you'll click directly on the certificate to mark where each piece of text should go — names, colleges, teams, or any other columns you have. You can preview every certificate before anything gets sent out.

Position the text fields

Click anywhere on the certificate to drop the active field. It automatically moves to the next field after each click — or click a column name in the table to select it manually.
